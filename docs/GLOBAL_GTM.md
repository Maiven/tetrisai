# Bandaepyeon — Global Go-To-Market
## Global Startup Career Decision Due Diligence
_Last updated: 2026-09-19_

## 1. Global thesis

A startup career decision is globally recognizable, but the evidence required to evaluate it is local.

The global product therefore has two layers:

**Universal ontology**
- Company viability
- Role reality
- Leadership & decision rights
- Cash & equity
- Learning & future options
- Evidence
- Flip conditions
- Verification actions

**Local context**
- country / region
- employment jurisdiction
- company incorporation / operating region
- equity plan terminology
- currency
- local tax/legal verification requirements
- local recruiting and startup-market norms

The product must never confuse localization with legal advice.

## 2. Why a global market exists

Startup Genome's GSER 2026 analyzes more than 5.5M companies across 350+ ecosystems.
The startup economy is global but increasingly concentrated by capital and AI capability.

Sources:
- https://startupgenome.com/report/the-global-startup-ecosystem-report-2026/state-of-the-global-startup-economy
- https://startupgenome.com/report/the-global-startup-ecosystem-report-2026/methodology

Carta's international compensation data also shows that equity practices differ by region.
APAC & Middle East employee equity pools, exercise rates, and workforce patterns are not identical to U.S. practices.

Source:
- https://carta.com/data/apac-me-equity-report/

This supports a universal decision framework with jurisdiction-aware questions rather than one universal answer.

## 3. Global beachhead

### First international ICP

English-speaking or English-fluent startup knowledge workers who:
- have 2–12 years experience
- work in Product, Engineering, Data/AI, Design, Growth, or commercial roles
- are evaluating Seed–Series C private-company opportunities
- receive meaningful equity or variable compensation
- face an offer decision within 3–14 days

### Highest-intent trigger

**"I have an actual startup offer in hand."**

This is globally easier to monetize than broad career anxiety because:
- the decision has a deadline
- compensation is concrete
- equity creates information asymmetry
- role/manager/company risk matters
- the cost of a bad decision is large

Levels.fyi currently sells:
- Startup Offer Evaluation: $250
- Startup negotiation package: $1,250

Source:
- https://www.levels.fyi/services/

Bandaepyeon should not compete as a human negotiation-coaching clone.
It should occupy the lower-cost, evidence-first due-diligence layer before or alongside human advice.

## 5. Product localization rules

### Never hard-code one country's equity system

Do not assume:
- U.S. ISO/NSO
- UK EMI
- Korean stock options
- Indian ESOP rules
- one tax treatment

Instead:
1. detect / ask region
2. identify the plan language actually present
3. explain what remains unknown
4. route regulated questions to official plan docs / local professionals

### Currency

Global pricing anchor:
- Free Decision Check
- $19 founding Deep Diligence Pass
- planned $29 standard price

Test purchasing-power-localized prices later.
Do not implement automatic geographic discrimination without validating customer response and legal/commercial implications.

## 8. Global moat

A global moat is not translation.

It is:

Startup Decision Ontology
+ jurisdiction-aware question generation
+ source provenance
+ Public Evidence Graph
+ user-verified evidence
+ outcome-linked Decision Graph
+ trust architecture
+ localized distribution

The long-term dataset should answer questions such as:
- Which unknowns most often change offer decisions by company stage?
- Which role claims are most often ambiguous?
- Which evidence questions are hardest for companies to answer?
- Which signals matter differently by region?
- Which assumptions were later regretted?

Raw private decision text should not be the asset.
The asset is the opt-in, privacy-preserving decision structure and outcome relationship.

---

## 9. Global trust architecture

### Product principle

**Your career decision is not your employer's dataset.**

Required before scaled paid launch:
- formal privacy policy
- formal terms
- subprocessors disclosure
- retention policy
- user deletion/export
- consent for cloud history
- incident-response process
- jurisdiction review for served markets
- consumer refund / cancellation rules by market

Do not claim compliance certification before it exists.
