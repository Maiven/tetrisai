# AX Research Synthesis 2026
## AI Transformation applied to Candidate Due Diligence

_Last reviewed: 2026-09-20_

This document synthesizes the most product-relevant 2025–2026 academic, government, and industry evidence reviewed for Bandaepyeon. It does **not** claim to exhaust every AX publication in existence.

---

# 1. Core conclusion

AX should not be measured as:

- "Does the company use AI?"
- "How many AI tools do employees have?"
- "How many agents were deployed?"

The decision-relevant question for a candidate is:

> **How has AI changed the work system I am about to enter?**

That means verifying:

1. work redesign
2. task-delegation depth
3. human–agent decision rights
4. organization readiness
5. skill trajectory
6. quality and accountability

Bandaepyeon therefore treats AX as a **role-reality overlay**, not as a company hype score or job-loss probability.

# 2. Government / multilateral evidence

## ILO — exposure is not a job-loss forecast

Generative AI and jobs: A 2025 update  
https://www.ilo.org/publications/generative-ai-and-jobs-2025-update

Workers’ exposure to AI: What indicators tell us – and what they don’t  
https://www.ilo.org/publications/workers%E2%80%99-exposure-ai-what-indicators-tell-us-%E2%80%93-and-what-they-don%E2%80%99t

Key implication:
- roughly one in four jobs globally has some GenAI exposure
- transformation is more likely than simple redundancy for many occupations
- exposure indicators must not be interpreted as direct forecasts of layoffs or employment outcomes

### Product rule
Never generate:
- "72% replacement probability"
- "safe from AI"
- "this job will disappear"

Instead identify which tasks, decisions, and handoffs may change and what evidence is still missing.

## OECD — skills and organizational capability are part of AI adoption

AI and skills: What we know so far (2026)  
https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/ai-and-skills_f843b352-en.html

Skills in the AI age (2026)  
https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/skills-in-the-ai-age_972bd15e-en.html

Key implications:
- lack of skills is an important barrier to AI adoption
- data analysis and interpretation become more important in AI-enabled work
- effects differ by sector, region, and skill level

### Product rule
AX diligence must ask not only what the model can do, but:
- what skills the employee is expected to develop
- whether the organization creates learning loops
- whether tools, data, management support, and governance exist

## Korea — AX is moving from adoption counts to task delegation

KIET, 서비스업 AI 전환 측정: 업무 위임 관점의 프레임워크 제안과 정책 활용 방안 (2026)  
https://www.kiet.re.kr/research/economyDetailView?detail_no=3237

The proposed delegation-depth framework:
- NON_AX — procedural execution; no material AI delegation confirmed
- LEVEL 1 — information processing delegated
- LEVEL 2 — task execution delegated
- LEVEL 3 — goal-based planning and execution delegated

The paper argues that AI adoption alone does not capture qualitative transformation.

### Product rule
Bandaepyeon uses **Delegation Depth** as a candidate-facing heuristic.
A higher level is not automatically "better." Higher delegation increases the importance of:
- oversight
- exception handling
- accountability
- quality controls
- skill retention

## Korea AI Basic Act — human oversight matters in high-impact contexts

AI Basic Act  
https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsInfoP.do?lsiSeq=282791

Article 34 includes duties concerning risk management, explanation, user protection, human management/oversight, and documentation for high-impact AI under applicable conditions.

### Product rule
Bandaepyeon does not assume every employer AI use falls under high-impact AI regulation.
However, when a role touches consequential automated decisions, the candidate should verify:
- human oversight
- accountability
- documentation
- escalation
- applicable legal obligations

# 3. Large-scale workplace and industry evidence

## Microsoft Work Trend Index 2026 — individual skill is not enough

https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/agents-human-agency-and-the-opportunity-for-every-organization

Reported findings include:
- organizational factors such as culture, manager support, governance, and talent practice are strongly associated with AI impact
- only a minority of AI users sit in the zone where individual capability and organizational readiness reinforce each other
- some workers are individually capable but blocked by their organizations

### Product rule
Add **Individual × Organization AX Fit**:
- potentially aligned
- individual ahead / organization may lag
- organization ahead / individual adaptation needed
- both early
- insufficient evidence

This is a diagnostic, not a score.

---

## NBER field experiment — tool access alone does not equal transformation

Shifting Work Patterns with Generative AI (2025, revised 2025)  
https://www.nber.org/papers/w33795

Field experiment:
- 66 firms
- 7,137 knowledge workers
- AI access reduced email time for active users
- individual tool provision did not produce clear shifts in task composition

### Product rule
Do not treat "we provide Copilot/ChatGPT" as proof of AX.
Ask:
- what workflow changed
- what handoff disappeared
- what task moved to AI
- what responsibility moved back to the human

## Deloitte 2026 — agentic AI exposes an operating-model gap

Rethinking operating models for humans with agents  
https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/topics/talent/operating-models-for-humans-ai-agents.html

The path to agentic transformation  
https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/industry/technology/path-to-agentic-transformation.html

Reported themes:
- many organizations expect substantial workflow redesign
- job redesign and workforce readiness lag deployment ambitions
- human-agent handoffs, accountability, governance, and quality assurance need explicit design

### Product rule
AX Role Reality must verify:
- which tasks agents perform
- which decisions humans retain
- who validates output
- who owns failures
- how exceptions escalate

## McKinsey 2026 — AI value requires operating-model redesign

State of AI 2026  
https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai

The operating model advantage  
https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/industrials/our-insights/the-operating-model-advantage-why-ai-winners-are-rewiring-their-organizations

Rewiring talent to value in the age of AI  
https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/people-and-organization/our-insights/rewiring-talent-to-value-in-the-age-of-ai

Core theme:
AI value depends on how organizations redesign work, roles, decisions, and capability—not on access to a model alone.

### Product rule
A candidate should verify:
- whether productivity expectations changed
- whether headcount assumptions changed
- whether decision rights changed
- whether quality standards changed
- whether role scope changed without corresponding authority/support

## PwC 2026 AI Jobs Barometer — skill change is accelerating

https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/1/services/ai/ai-jobs-barometer.html

Reported findings:
- AI-exposed jobs show faster skill change
- AI-specific skills carry substantial wage premiums in the analyzed data
- roles can move toward greater human expertise/judgment or become easier for non-experts depending on how AI changes the work

### Product rule
Do not ask only "Is this job automated?"
Ask:
- which human judgment becomes more valuable
- which expertise becomes commoditized
- whether the user is building durable career capital

---

## Stanford AI Index 2026 — adoption is broad, impact is uneven

https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report/economy

Key themes:
- corporate AI adoption has risen substantially
- agent deployment remains early in many business functions
- productivity gains vary by task structure and implementation
- younger workers in exposed roles may face different hiring effects

### Product rule
Never generalize aggregate AI effects to one user or one startup.
Keep AX claims evidence-specific.

---

## Anthropic Economic Index — automation and augmentation coexist

https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-june-2026-report

The index distinguishes automation and augmentation patterns and tracks changing forms of agentic use.

### Product rule
Bandaepyeon uses an **exposureMode**:
- augmentation-leaning
- automation-leaning
- mixed
- insufficient evidence

This is a description of the current evidence, not a forecast of job survival.

# 4. AX Role Reality model in Bandaepyeon

## A. Work redesign
Ask:
- Which workflow steps disappeared?
- Which new steps were created?
- Which handoffs moved from human-to-human to human-to-agent?

## B. Delegation depth
Classify only when evidence exists:
- NON_AX
- L1 information processing
- L2 bounded task execution
- L3 goal-based planning/execution

## C. Organization readiness
Verify:
- sanctioned tools
- data access
- governance
- manager support
- reusable workflows
- evaluation standards

## D. Human judgment and agency
Verify:
- final approval
- exception handling
- escalation
- accountability
- decision rights

## E. Skill trajectory
Verify:
- what becomes more valuable
- what becomes commoditized
- what the candidate will be able to prove in 12 months
- whether AI use creates learning or skill atrophy

## F. Quality and accountability
Verify:
- output QA
- evaluation
- incident handling
- human owner
- auditability where required

# 5. AX-specific anti-patterns

Never treat these as evidence by themselves:
- "AI-first"
- "AI-native"
- "agentic"
- access to ChatGPT/Copilot
- number of AI licenses
- one internal AI demo
- one productivity claim without method
- one job-loss estimate

Never output:
- employee replacement probability
- company AX score without a validated measurement model
- deterministic "AI-safe career" labels

# 7. Product thesis after AX research

Bandaepyeon should not answer:

> "Will AI replace me?"

It should help the user answer:

> **"What work am I actually being hired to do after AI changes this team, which decisions will remain mine, what will be delegated to agents, what skills will I build, and who is accountable when the system is wrong?"**

That is a materially better career-diligence question.
